\begin{abstract}
We specify a source-faithful Lean 4 formalization of the
Bombieri--Vinogradov theorem in the level-of-distribution form used by modern
bounded-prime-gap arguments.  This document fixes the target statement, its
relationship to the literature, and the intended large-sieve proof
architecture.  The definitions and target proposition compile, but the
Bombieri--Vinogradov proof itself remains open at this stage.
\end{abstract}

\maketitle

\section{Introduction}

The Bombieri--Vinogradov theorem says, roughly, that primes are equidistributed
on average among reduced residue classes for moduli almost as large as
$x^{1/2}$.  It replaces an unavailable pointwise estimate of comparable
strength with an averaged result and is a central input to sieve arguments.
In particular, the level-of-distribution formulation below is the hypothesis
used in Maynard's bounded-prime-gap framework \cite{Maynard2015} and in the
downstream Lean project \cite{PrimeGapsLib}.

The present project targets the analytic theorem itself.  It does not reprove
the bounded-gap consequence, which is already the responsibility of the
downstream development.

\section{Statement of the problem}

For $x\in\mathbb R$, let $\pi(x)$ be the number of primes at most $x$.
For $q\in\mathbb N$ and $a\in(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^\times$, let
$\pi(x;q,a)$ be the number of primes $p\leq x$ whose residue modulo $q$ is
$a$, and let $\varphi(q)$ be Euler's totient.

\begin{theorem}[Bombieri--Vinogradov target]\label{thm:bv}
For every $\theta\in\mathbb R$ with $\theta<\tfrac12$ and every
$A\in\mathbb R$ with $A\geq1$, there is a $c\in\mathbb R$, $c>0$, such that
for every $x\in\mathbb R$, $x\geq3$,
\[
  \sum_{1\leq q\leq\lfloor x^\theta\rfloor}
  \max_{a\in(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^\times}
  \left|\pi(x;q,a)-\frac{\pi(x)}{\varphi(q)}\right|
  \leq \frac{c x}{(\log x)^A}.
\]
\end{theorem}

The strict inequality $\theta<\tfrac12$, the lower bounds $A\geq1$ and
$x\geq3$, and the inclusive modulus cutoff are part of the formal target.

\section{Relation to the literature}

The theorem originates in independent work of Bombieri \cite{Bombieri1965}
and A.~I. Vinogradov \cite{Vinogradov1965}.  Our exact quantifier surface is
adapted from equation~(1.3) of Maynard \cite{Maynard2015}, with endpoints
matched to the challenge statement in \texttt{PrimeGapsLib} commit
\path{1faa7b14e82ddebc2772dfb9153922f01b106477} \cite{PrimeGapsLib}.

For the proof, Vaughan's large-sieve lecture notes \cite{Vaughan2025} are the
primary working exposition.  Harper's elementary-methods notes
\cite{Harper2015} provide supplementary analytic-number-theory background.
These expository sources guide the proof organization; they are not claimed as
the origin of the theorem.

\section{Proof architecture}

The selected route is the classical large-sieve proof.  The dependency graph
is organized from stable, reusable analytic components toward the final
statement:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item prove the primitive Dirichlet-character large-sieve inequality;
  \item formalize Vaughan's identity for the von Mangoldt function;
  \item combine these in a mean-value estimate for character sums;
  \item isolate the Siegel--Walfisz input for small moduli;
  \item prove a weighted Bombieri--Vinogradov estimate for the Chebyshev sum;
  \item remove prime powers and partial-sum weights to obtain
        Theorem~\ref{thm:bv} for $\pi(x;q,a)$.
\end{enumerate}

Independent branches are intended to depend on small definition and helper
modules, not on one another.  The final assembly module will import the branch
results only after their interfaces have stabilized.  The first live proof
obligation is item~1.

\section{Formalization and verification}

The target declaration and source module are
\begin{center}
\texttt{BombieriVinogradov.Statement}\\
\texttt{BombieriVinogradov/Definitions/Statement.lean}.
\end{center}
Its supporting definitions are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{Real.primeCountingZMod};
  \item \texttt{BombieriVinogradov.primeCounting};
  \item \texttt{BombieriVinogradov.primeDiscrepancy};
  \item \texttt{BombieriVinogradov.averagePrimeDiscrepancy}.
\end{itemize}

The repository is pinned to Lean~4.33.1 and the matching mathlib release.  At
this stage only the definitions and proposition are kernel checked.  The
public \texttt{Challenge.lean} and \texttt{Solution.lean} files deliberately
expose a project-specific research placeholder, not Theorem~\ref{thm:bv}.
They will be replaced by the real theorem surface only after the proof compiles.
Consequently, current Comparator or NanoDa success is infrastructure evidence,
not evidence that Bombieri--Vinogradov has been proved.

\section{Limitations and review status}

No proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:bv} is present yet.  In particular, the character
large sieve, Vaughan identity, Siegel--Walfisz estimate, weighted theorem, and
conversion to prime counts remain open.  The target statement has been checked
against Maynard's formulation and the cited downstream Lean surface, but it has
not received independent expert mathematical review.  Automated builds and
source-policy checks do not substitute for such review.
